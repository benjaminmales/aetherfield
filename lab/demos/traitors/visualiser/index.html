Results are POSTed to the cloud on Finish, which messages registered guests.
How to play: click Connect to gateway and pick the USB serial port. Press Start voting. Bands raise their wrist to cast a vote for the currently highlighted traitor — tap the arrow to switch to the other. With split vote off (default), each band can vote at most once. Press Finish to reveal the winner; the live signals panel below will then show real-time hand-raise and clap state for every band.

Noise filtering: the gateway runs in promiscuous mode and can briefly mis-parse unrelated 802.15.4 frames as fake bands. Trust band after N packets drops the one-shots — a real band passes the threshold in ~1.5 s at the 500 ms broadcast cadence. If a stubborn non-band keeps reappearing with the same uuid, paste it into Ignore uuids (comma-separated, hex). The header pill shows how many candidates are currently being suppressed.

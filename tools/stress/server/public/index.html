Proximity

near mid far

Each device is attributed to the gateway that hears it loudest, and bucketed by how loud it is there. No coordinates are claimed — RSSI moves 10 dB when a person turns around, which is fatal to a position fix and survivable for an ordering. The gateway hearing a device most strongly is almost always the nearest one, so carrying a board across the room moves it between bars.

Load

Light

In op mode 0 the param is the beacon interval: param × 100 ms, 0 meaning the 1 s default. 100 = 10 s, 10 = 1 s, 3 = 300 ms. That is how you sweep offered load — it re-rates every virtual device on every board at the same instant, over the air, and saves opening an RTT session per board. Set the matching interval above afterwards or the delivery figure is measured against the wrong expectation.

The boards run the shipping aura_band, so this is the real command path: real dedupe, real group matching, real animation engine. Their RGB LEDs obey it. Watch CMD adoption climb as devices echo the sequence they acted on — that is the delivery statistic, and the room changing colour is the same fact visible from across it.

Calibration

Measure both before trusting any distance: put one device exactly 1 m from a gateway and read its RSSI, then move it to 10 m and solve for n. Every estimate on the map scales with these two numbers, and the defaults are textbook values, not your venue.
